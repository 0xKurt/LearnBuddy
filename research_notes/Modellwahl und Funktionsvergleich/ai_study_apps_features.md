## 1. Features per app: target age, business model and price, answers vs. teaching

### Takeaway

The market has split in two:

- **Answer engines:** Gauth, Photomath, Mathway, Numerade, Brainly. Their business is photo → solution, with the step-by-step explanation behind a paywall.
- **Study systems:** Knowunity, StudySmarter/Vaia, simpleclub, Quizlet. They add AI chat, flashcards and quizzes generated from uploaded materials, exam plans and streaks.

Since mid-2025, every general chatbot (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) has shipped an opt-in "study / learning" mode that uses Socratic questioning. In all of them the student can switch it off, so it is a toggle, not a guarantee. Khanmigo is the only large product that is built from the start never to give direct answers.

### Cited Findings

**Knowunity**

- KI-Tutor: answers follow-up questions in context, quizzes the student, creates summaries and extra exercises. **Photomath and Gauth**

- Photomath is owned by Google and covers only math. Flow: scan → answer → multi-step explanation, often with several methods [S] — [bbntimes](https://www.bbntimes.com/technology/gauth-vs-photomath-the-definitive-battle-of-ai-homework-helpers-in-2025), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photomath).

  - Some US schools block it on school networks.
  - Sources [S; implicator.ai is secondary, Forbes is the stronger source]: [Forbes 2024](https://www.forbes.com/sites/emilybaker-white/2024/04/03/gauth-bytedance-tiktok-homework-app/), [implicator.ai](https://www.implicator.ai/bytedances-homework-app-gauthmath-quietly-conquers-american-classrooms/)

**Studdy**

- Voice and text chat. Free plan: 5 scans/day plus unlimited chat tutoring [S] — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.casmify.studdy&hl=en-US). I found no evidence of a parent dashboard.

### Inferences

- [I] Almost every German competitor (Knowunity, StudySmarter, simpleclub) has several tabs: notes, flashcards, planner, community, tutor. LearnBuddy's single chat screen is a clear point of difference, but it has to provide flashcards, quizzes and a plan as outputs inside the chat, not as screens.
- [I] The pricing norm for German school students is freemium plus about €7–15/month. Students react badly to ads and to paywalls on content (Knowunity) or on explanations (Mathway). A paywall on the "teaching" part is the most resented pattern.
- [I] Every "study mode" in a general chatbot can be switched off by the student. LearnBuddy's rule that the tutor never gives away answers is enforced in code (policy, not prompt). That is a real difference, and parents are likely to value it.
- [I] Discontinued products (Q-Chat, the Socratic app) show that a stand-alone AI tutor chat inside a flashcard app did not keep users. Tutoring works when it is tied to the student's own materials and goals.

## 2. Trends 2025–2026

### Takeaway

Seven trends, each with its own evidence below:

1. **"Study mode" in general chatbots.** All four big providers shipped one between April 2025 and November 2025.
2. **Voice tutors.** Gauth "Emma", Duolingo Video Call, Khanmigo speech.
3. **Camera homework help.** The Socratic app moved into Google Lens; Gauth covers every subject.
4. **Spaced repetition personalised by a model.** Quizlet Memory Score.
5. **Exam prep from the student's own materials.** Quizlet Magic Notes, Brainly, Vaia, NotebookLM.
6. **Parent controls.** ChatGPT Sept 2025, Khanmigo parent accounts, Knowunity parent Pro.
7. **AI tasks assigned by teachers.** Gems and NotebookLM in Google Classroom since Sept 2025.

### Inferences

- [I] The market has moved toward what LearnBuddy already does: grounding in the student's own material plus a Socratic stance. The difference now lies in how well it is done (proactive planning, one surface, code-enforced no-answer policy), not in having the feature.
- [I] Teacher-assigned Gems grounded in class materials are the strongest structural competitor in schools that use Google. LearnBuddy covers this from the student side (worksheet photo → practice) without needing the teacher.
- [I] Parent dashboards on the market show study time and topics. LearnBuddy's rule that learners never see counts of due items or missed days is compatible with that. A parent view could show topics and upcoming exams without shaming the child. This is not researched, only a design hypothesis.

### Gaps

- No source found on how widely voice tutors are used, or how well they work, for 10–14-year-olds.
- No source found on whether Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude voice modes work together with study mode.

## 3. What students and parents praise and complain about

### Takeaway

**Praise** (snippet evidence): explanations that are understandable and faster than a human; a strong German content base (Knowunity); AI answers some parents rate above ChatGPT.

**Complaints:**

- ads and subscriptions that come in suddenly after a free period (Knowunity);
- paywalled steps (Mathway), and billing traps with auto-renewal (Numerade);
- wrong AI or community answers (Knowunity, Brainly);
- the answer-copying and cheating image of photo solvers (Gauth);
- privacy, especially ByteDance for Gauth.

Common Sense survey data suggests most teens use AI to get answers, while also reporting that it helps them understand.

### Cited Findings

- **German App Store reviews for Knowunity** (as summarised in the snippet):
  - "jetzt viel Werbung" (now lots of ads);
  - a €9/month subscription called very expensive, "da die App-Entwickler die Lernzettel nicht selbst hochladen" (because the developers do not write the notes themselves);
  - the Pro lock on student-made notes called "Geldmacherei";
  - some parents say the AI answers are better than ChatGPT.

## 4. Which features improve learning, and which only drive engagement

### Takeaway

- **AI answers without guardrails hurt later performance.** The Bastani et al. PNAS 2025 field experiment found that a Socratic, hint-based tutor avoided the harm but did not raise exam scores above control. OECD PISA 2025 links daily use of AI to draft texts with a 28-point lower science score, correlational only.
- **Retrieval practice and spacing** are the best-supported study techniques (Dunlosky 2013).
- **Gamification** shows positive average effects in meta-analyses, but these depend heavily on design. Leaderboards can raise anxiety.
- **Streaks and notifications** have strong evidence for retention. The evidence that they cause learning mostly comes from vendors.

### Cited Findings

**Bastani et al., PNAS 2025**

- ~1,000 high-school math students. Two arms: "GPT Base" (a ChatGPT-like interface) and "GPT Tutor" (teacher-designed hints, no answers) [S] — [PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422633122), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40560616/).
- During practice, performance improved by 48% (Base) and 127% (Tutor). When access was removed, Base students underperformed, which the authors read as reduced skill acquisition. Tutor students scored about the same as control, so the guardrails mitigated the harm [S] — [PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422633122).

**OECD, PISA 2025 and Digital Education Outlook 2026**

- Students who never or almost never use AI to draft writing assignments scored 509 in science. Daily users scored 481. The gap of 28 points (adjusted for socio-economic status) is about 1.5 years of schooling [S, press summary of OECD] — [Yahoo/OECD](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/school-students-who-use-ai-get-worse-test-scores-oecd-warns-035448706.html), [OECD PISA 2025 Vol I](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2025-results-volume-i_73451bc5-en/full-report/student-school-life-and-beyond_861e5904.html).
- Students who use AI often **and** learn at school to assess the quality of AI output score slightly _higher_. Advantaged students report this more often, a possible new divide [S].
- Students spend about 1.4 hours a day on homework (OECD average) [S].
- Digital Education Outlook 2026: GenAI supports learning when guided by pedagogical principles. Without that, outsourcing tasks "enhances performance with no real learning gains" [S] — [OECD DEO 2026](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_062a7394-en.html).

**Google Sierra Leone RCT** (vendor-reported; see §1)

- 1,763 junior-secondary students, Guided Learning ≥12 hours over 8 weeks: 50th → 64th percentile in math [S] — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/guided-learning/).

**Retrieval and spacing**

- Dunlosky et al. 2013 rate practice testing and distributed practice as the only two "high utility" techniques [S] — [Dunlosky et al., PSPI 2013](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1529100612453266).
- A later meta-analysis (242 studies, 169,179 participants) concludes that distributed practice and practice testing are the most effective. It holds across ages and test formats [S] — [Frontiers in Education 2021](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2021.581216/full).
- Retrieval is stronger when spaced [S] — [Evidence Based Education](https://evidencebased.education/resource/retrieval-and-spaced-practice-study-strategies-that-must-be-combined/).

**Gamification**

- Meta-analyses report positive average effects (e.g. g = 0.822 across 41 studies, >5,000 participants). Effects depend on design, duration (3–6 months was best) and setting [S] — [PMC meta-analysis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10591086/), [ScienceDirect 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1747938X19302908).
- Some controlled studies found no gain in knowledge from competitive gamification, and leaderboards may raise anxiety [S, secondary blog] — [StudyPulse](https://studypulse.education/blog/gamification-in-education-what-research-says/).

**Streaks and notifications**

- Duolingo data: learners with a 7-day streak are 2.4× more likely to return the next day. Streaks correlate with subscribing. These are engagement and retention metrics, not learning [S, vendor data via secondary sources] — [Quartr](https://quartr.com/insights/edge/keeping-the-streak-alive-the-story-of-duolingo), [StriveCloud](https://www.strivecloud.io/blog/gamification-examples-boost-user-retention-duolingo).
- Academic work on streaks is mostly habit or case studies (Oulu thesis; IJMAT case study) [S] — [Oulu](https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/54117/nbnfioulu-202502121605.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y), [IJMAT](https://www.wr-publishing.org/index.php/ijmat/article/view/908).

**Newer RCT not assessed**

- A July 2026 arXiv paper, "Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI" (arXiv 2607.08849), appeared in results. The fetch was blocked, so its content is not assessed [S, title only] — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08849).

### Inferences

- [I] This evidence supports LearnBuddy's core design:
  - Socratic tutoring with hints and no answers, enforced in code (Bastani);
  - practice prepared from the student's own worksheets and exams (retrieval);
  - Buddy getting in touch at spaced intervals before exams (spacing);
  - teaching the student to check what the AI says (the PISA finding on AI literacy).
- [I] Streaks and counts are engagement tools with a documented downside (anxiety, shame). They are consistent with LearnBuddy's rule never to show due-item or missed-day counts. If any continuity cue is used, it should be positive and not based on loss aversion. This is a design hypothesis, not evidence.
- [I] Bastani's "Tutor" arm only _neutralised_ the harm; it did not beat control. Hints alone are not enough. Actual retrieval practice without AI help, such as short checks where Buddy does not assist, is probably what produces the learning gain.
- [T, not re-verified] From memory: Bastani's GPT Base arm scored about 17% lower than control on the unassisted exam, and the setting was a Turkish high school, grades 9–11. Check this in the PNAS paper before citing.
- [T, not re-verified] From memory: Sailer & Homner (2020, Educational Psychology Review) found g ≈ 0.49 for cognitive outcomes of gamification. This is more conservative than the g = 0.82 above.

### Gaps

- No causal study found specifically on AI homework helpers for ages 10–14 (Bastani is high school; the PISA figures are for 15-year-olds and correlational).
- No independent study found on push notifications and learning outcomes in children.

## 5. Safety features for minors, incidents and regulatory actions

### Takeaway

**Safety features in the market:**

- age gates (ChatGPT, Copilot: 13+; school accounts need an admin to switch access on);
- age prediction that defaults to the under-18 experience;
- parental linking, blackout hours and distress alerts (ChatGPT);
- teacher-grounded, school-managed access (Gemini Classroom).

**Regulators:**

- Italy's Garante fined Replika €5M (May 2025) and Character.AI €158K (July 2026) over missing or weak age verification;
- the US FTC amended the COPPA Rule (April 2025) to limit keeping children's data to improve algorithms and to require consent before sharing with third parties;
- I found no FTC or Garante action against an edtech homework app specifically.

### Cited Findings

- **ChatGPT (Sept 2025):**
  - parental controls with account linking, blackout hours, feature disabling and acute-distress notifications;
  - an under-18 experience that blocks graphic and sexual content and flirting, and handles self-harm topics separately;
  - age prediction that defaults to the under-18 experience when unsure.
  - Sources [S]: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/16/openai-chatgpt-teens-parent.html), [OpenAI age prediction](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-age-prediction/), [OpenAI parental controls](https://openai.com/index/introducing-parental-controls/)
- **Microsoft:** Study and Learn is for ages 13+, and teen Copilot access has to be enabled by the admin [S] — [MS Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/education/copilot/study-learn-agent), [MS Edu blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/education/blog/2025/05/empowering-teen-students-to-achieve-more-with-copilot-chat-and-microsoft-365-copilot/).
- **Google:** Gemini and NotebookLM are available to under-18 students through school accounts. NotebookLM is grounded "only in the materials educators upload" [S] — [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2025/09/educators-create-gems-notebooks-google-classroom.html), [datastudios](https://www.datastudios.org/post/google-expands-gemini-ai-access-to-schools-and-students-under-18).

  - Amended COPPA Rule, April 2025:

### Inferences

- [I] The regulators' main focus in 2025–2026 is companion or roleplay chatbots and age verification, not homework tutors. - [I] The COPPA 2025 rule on voiceprints and on not keeping data for model training is relevant to any voice feature LearnBuddy adds. The equivalent in the EU/Germany is GDPR Art. 8 plus the AI Act (not researched here).
- [I] LearnBuddy already uses a server-side PIN for adults loosening contact settings and treats contact as opt-in. This matches or exceeds the market pattern of parent linking and blackout hours.
